Ojos y oídos.

Turn on the microphone so the work hears your claps and stamps, and the camera so it sees you dance. Everything stays on this device: nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

With speakers, the painting can’t tell its own claps from yours, so guide claps are muted while it listens. With AirPods or other Bluetooth headphones, choose your computer’s or phone’s built-in microphone as the input: headphone mics filter out claps.

Sobre la obra.

Palmas nace de querer aprender el compás sin tenerlo todavía. Un tejido de hilos que vibra: con cada palma y cada pisada, el sonido levanta unas manos y un pie desde el tejido. Cuanto más a compás, más luz.

Palmas began as a wish to learn compás before I had it. A woven field of threads that vibrates: with every clap and stamp, the sound raises hands and a foot out of the weave. The closer to compás, the more light.

Sofía Odeh, 2026. The hidden shapes come from my painted studies; the threads, the listening and the code were built with Claude. The microphone and camera run only on your device.
