An interactive painting that listens to your palmas, watches you dance, and teaches you the compás.

Ojos y oídos.

Turn on the microphone so the painting hears your claps and stamps, and the camera so it sees you dance. Everything stays on this device: nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

With speakers, the painting can’t tell its own claps from yours, so guide claps are muted while it listens. With AirPods or other Bluetooth headphones, choose your computer’s or phone’s built-in microphone as the input: headphone mics filter out claps.

Tus palmasteach it your claps

Clap 4 claras (fingers on palm, sharp), then 4 sordas (cupped, soft), then stamp 4 times.

Sincronizartiming

Speakers only: the painting plays 8 clicks and listens to measure its own delay. Or tap along with heel beats.

Aprende el compás.

Watch the hands and the dancer, then join in: clap, stamp, tap the painting, or press Space.

Sobre la obra.

Palmas nace de querer aprender el compás sin tenerlo todavía. Unas manos pintadas que te esperan, una bailaora que pisa contigo, y el ritmo de Camarón como maestro.

Palmas began as a wish to learn compás before I had it. Painted hands that wait for you, a dancer who stamps with you, and Camarón’s rhythm as the teacher.

Sofía Odeh, 2026. Paintings made with AI image tools from my own direction; animation, listening and code built with Claude. The microphone and camera run only on your device.
