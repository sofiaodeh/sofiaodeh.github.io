NEST: Lunar Exploration

SEDS Mechanical Lead  ·  Apr 2024 – Dec 2025

Networked mini-COBRA robots for lunar surface exploration

Project Overview

Led the mechanical design for networked mini-COBRA robots designed for advanced lunar surface exploration missions. This project focused on developing robust, autonomous robotic systems capable of coordinated operation as a networked team.

My Role

As mechanical lead for SEDS (Students for the Exploration and Development of Space), I led the team developing networked mini-COBRA modules for coordinated lunar exploration. I led the CAD redesign and prototyping for untethered battery integration and autonomous operation, and carried out the 3D printing (SLA and FDM), assembly, and testing to improve module scalability.

The project built on my 2023 summer at Northeastern's Silicon Synapse Lab, where I worked on COBRA, a bio-inspired snake-like exploration robot for crater observation, and integrated Raspberry Pi sensors via ROS for obstacle detection and autonomous navigation.
